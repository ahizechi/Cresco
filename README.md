# Cresco

**Build better habits. Get a clearer picture of your money.**

Cresco brings your daily habits and personal finances together in one Windows app. Check in on the things you want to do more often, see where your money goes, and keep track of what you're working towards.

Your records stay on your computer. No account to create, no subscription, and no ads.

## Get Cresco

[**Download Cresco for Windows**](https://github.com/ahizechi/Cresco/releases/latest)

Choose the file ending in **x64-setup.exe**, run it, and open Cresco. The installer handles the setup for you.

Cresco checks for updates automatically. When an update is available, you can install it and restart from the app. Your habits, finances and settings stay with you.

## Make your habits stick

- Set up daily habits or choose the days that work for you.
- Track a simple check-in or a measured target, like pages read or minutes spent.
- Add notes, review your history, and watch your streaks grow.
- Pause or archive a habit when life changes.
- Use ongoing timers and streaks for routines you want to keep going.

## Know where your money goes

- Keep your accounts and transactions in one place.
- Import bank statements from CSV files and review them before adding anything.
- Organise spending with categories and remembered merchant rules.
- Set budgets, track savings goals, and plan upcoming bills and income.
- Record transfers and check your balances against your statements.

Accounts keep their own currencies. The overall money view totals your GBP accounts, so different currencies aren't mixed together. Bank accounts aren't connected automatically; you add transactions yourself or import a statement.

## Your information stays yours

Your habits and financial records are stored locally and encrypted for your Windows account. Cresco works offline for everyday tracking and doesn't upload your records or collect analytics.

You can export a backup from Settings and restore it later. Exported backups contain your records, so keep them somewhere private. Cresco also keeps recovery copies to help protect your saved information.

## A little room to make it your own

Choose a light or dark theme, navigate with your keyboard, and reduce motion if you prefer a quieter interface.
